Privacy

Jarito has no accounts and no database. Here is everything that happens to your data.

What stays on your device

Your calendar feed link, your assignment list, and what you’ve marked done or started are saved in your browser’s local storage. They never leave that browser except as described below. Clearing site data, or pressing Disconnect calendar, removes the link.

What passes through our server

Canvas and Brightspace don’t let websites read calendar feeds directly, so when Jarito checks your calendar it sends your feed link to a small function at /api/feed. That function downloads the calendar from your school’s system and hands it straight back to your browser. It doesn’t save the link or the calendar, and doesn’t write them to logs. Your IP address is used briefly to limit how often a single device can sync.

Moving to your phone

The “Open on my phone” code puts your feed link after a # in the address. Browsers never send that part of an address to a server, and Jarito removes it from the address bar as soon as it has saved the link.

What we don’t do

No analytics, no ads, no cookies, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared. Jarito is open source, so you can check all of this in the code on GitHub.

Feedback

If you use the feedback form, your message, plus your school and email only if you fill them in, is sent as an email to the person who maintains Jarito. It isn’t stored anywhere else or connected to your calendar. The form refuses messages containing a calendar feed link, so a link can’t be sent by accident.

Support payments

Jarito is free for students, and chipping in is optional. It doesn’t change how Jarito works or what it stores. If you choose to support it, the payment happens on Stripe’s checkout page: Jarito never sees your card details, and nothing about your payment is connected to your calendar or assignments. The charge appears as Oduor Web Services.

Your feed link

Anyone who has your feed link can see your course calendar. If you think it has leaked, reset it: in Canvas, Calendar, then Calendar Feed, then reset; in Brightspace, Calendar, then Subscribe, then reset the token and paste the new one into Jarito.

Jarito is an independent open source student tool and is not affiliated with Instructure, Canvas, D2L or Brightspace. Questions and feedback can be sent through the GitHub project.
