Privacy

Jarito has no accounts and no database. Here is everything that happens to your data.

What stays on your device

Your Canvas feed link, your assignment list, and what you’ve marked done or started are saved in your browser’s local storage. They never leave that browser except as described below. Clearing site data, or pressing Disconnect Canvas, removes the link.

What passes through our server

Canvas doesn’t let websites read calendar feeds directly, so when Jarito checks your calendar it sends your feed link to a small function at /api/feed. That function downloads the calendar from Canvas and hands it straight back to your browser. It doesn’t save the link or the calendar, and doesn’t write them to logs. Your IP address is used briefly to limit how often a single device can sync.

Moving to your phone

The “Open on my phone” code puts your feed link after a # in the address. Browsers never send that part of an address to a server, and Jarito removes it from the address bar as soon as it has saved the link.

What we don’t do

No analytics, no ads, no cookies, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared. Jarito is open source, so you can check all of this in the code on GitHub.

Your feed link

Anyone who has your feed link can see your Canvas calendar. If you think it has leaked, reset it in Canvas (Calendar, then Calendar Feed, then reset) and paste the new one into Jarito.
