Brightspace / D2L setup

Turn on calendar feeds, then copy the Subscribe link.

The best link is All Calendars and Tasks, because that includes every course instead of one class at a time.

Paste my link I use Canvas

Steps

  1. Open Brightspace in a browser.
  2. Go to Calendar.
  3. Click Settings.
  4. Turn on Enable Calendar Feeds, then save.
  5. Click Subscribe.
  6. Choose All Calendars and Tasks.
  7. Copy the whole link and paste it into Jarito.

No Subscribe button? Open Calendar Settings, tick Enable Calendar Feeds, save, and it appears.

Should I choose one course or all courses?

Choose All Calendars and Tasks unless you only want Jarito to watch one course.

Something looks wrong for my school.

Schools set Brightspace up in slightly different ways. Tell us through the feedback form, describing what looks off (never your feed link), and it will get fixed.
