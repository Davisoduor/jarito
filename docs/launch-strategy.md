# Jarito launch strategy

Jarito is not another planner. It is the small watcher students open to see whether a deadline moved.

## Positioning

**Promise:** Never miss a moved deadline again.

**Audience:** Students whose school uses Canvas, Brightspace or D2L.

**Primary action:** Paste a calendar feed link and start watching.

**Why it can spread:** It solves one painful job, works without an account, and classmates can share it without school approval.

## What to measure first

- 100 students using Jarito across 5 campuses.
- 20 students saying it caught a moved deadline or made the week easier to read.
- 10 Brightspace users confirming titles, course names and due times are correct.
- 1 student resource page, tutoring center or club newsletter linking to Jarito.
- 1 post from a student who shared it without being asked.

## Feedback questions

Ask early users:

1. What school and LMS are you using?
2. Did the setup directions get you to the right link?
3. Did the assignment titles and course names look right?
4. Were any due dates or times wrong?
5. Did Jarito catch a moved deadline?
6. What would make you open it every day?
7. What was confusing enough that you almost quit?

Never ask people to send their feed link. A screenshot of the assignment list is enough.

## Outreach channels

- Class group chats.
- Campus Discords.
- School subreddits.
- First year student resource pages.
- Tutoring centers.
- Disability and accessibility offices.
- Student government newsletters.
- Professors who already remind students about Canvas calendar feeds.

## Short post

I made a free tool that watches Canvas and Brightspace for moved deadlines. Paste your calendar feed once, and it shows what changed since the last time you checked. No account, no database, open source.

Try it: https://jarito.vercel.app

## Group chat version

Made this because Canvas quietly moves dates and it is easy to miss. Jarito puts everything due in one list and flags moved deadlines. Free, no login: https://jarito.vercel.app

## Institution angle, later

Do not start with procurement. Start with student usage and proof.

When there is usage across several campuses, approach:

- Student success offices.
- Academic advising.
- Accessibility services.
- Tutoring centers.
- Orientation teams.

Lead with privacy and support load:

Jarito helps students notice changed deadlines without storing school data, requiring an account, or asking IT to install an LTI integration.

## Phone app decision

Stay PWA first. Build a native app only when:

- Weekly active use is strong.
- Students repeatedly ask for push notifications.
- Setup and retention are already working on the web.
